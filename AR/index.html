Maquette AR Overlay

Loads your scan as a semi-transparent ghost in passthrough AR so you can compare it live against the physical maquette.

Right trigger (hold): drag the model to follow your controller
Right thumbstick (left/right): rotate the model
Left thumbstick (up/down): scale the model
A button: cycle look — translucent / wireframe / solid
B button: reset position in front of you
